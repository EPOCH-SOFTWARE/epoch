Five ways the mark can carry what EPOCH believes. Each one uses the same bold wordmark; only the idea in the symbol changes.

Option A

Zero point

Today’s logo.

A ring open at the top, with its starting point in the gap. In computing, the epoch is time zero, and one complete pass starts and ends right there.

Option B

The overrun

Recommended: it says your belief out loud.

One complete pass, and then it keeps going. The line runs past where it started and ends in the orange point. You don’t stop at the finish line or the scope of the contract.

Option C

The extra mile

Typographic, quiet, and very hard to copy.

The name itself carries the idea: the H’s crossbar runs past the letter and ends in the orange point. No separate symbol needed. It reads as “EPOCH, going further”.

Works best as a wordmark; pair it with mark B or D for the app icon.

Option D

In focus

Matches the hover brackets used across the site.

Four corner brackets frame one point, like a camera finding focus or an engineer selecting a part. It stands for attention that doesn’t drift: nothing important outside the frame.

Option E

New era

The most ambitious, and the boldest in colour.

A half-sun rising inside the circle. A new epoch is the start of a new era, for EPOCH and for the companies it builds AI with.
